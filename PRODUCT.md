# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

A mix of three audiences, confirmed by the owner:

- **Evaluators**: people at a company deciding whether Rosetta is worth adopting. They arrive cold, usually without an InDesign file to hand, and need to understand what it does within seconds and judge a real result in the browser.
- **Production / localization staff**: people at publishers or schools who need InDesign documents in other languages and check the output before it goes to print. They come back repeatedly and work through flagged items.
- **InDesign designers**: designers or agencies who know InDesign well, open the translated file, and fix the remaining layout details themselves.

Design for the evaluator's first visit, and keep the progress and results screens useful for repeated production work.

## Product Purpose

Rosetta translates the live text inside Adobe InDesign `.idml` files into other languages (Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic) while keeping the formatting, fitting the translated text back into the layout, setting Arabic right-to-left, and producing a QA report. Success means a person can upload a file (or try the built-in sample), watch it run, compare original and translation side by side in the browser, and download a file that opens in InDesign, knowing exactly what was adjusted and what still needs a human.

## Positioning

Rosetta works inside the document's own structure rather than on extracted text. It translates whole sentences with the formatting runs tagged so bold and italic land on the right words. It measures translated text with real font metrics against each frame's real geometry, then re-wraps, shrinks slightly, or shortens the wording, and flags what it cannot fit instead of hiding it. It returns an InDesign file, not a document in another format.

## Operating Context

- Input: an `.idml` exported from InDesign, or a `.zip` with the `.idml` and its Links folder.
- A job runs in the background for seconds to a few minutes; the visitor watches a step checklist.
- Results are read in the browser (summary, side-by-side preview by page and story, flags) and downloaded (InDesign package, `.idml`, QA report, JSON).
- Runs privately (password) or as a public demo with rate limits, a word limit and a daily spending cap; uploads can be paused while the sample still works.
- Files are deleted after 24 hours by default.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack is fixed: Flask + Jinja templates + plain JavaScript, no build step; served under a strict Content-Security-Policy (no inline styles or scripts; fonts self-hosted).
- Pipeline modules and `app.py` logic are out of scope for UI work.
- The JavaScript hooks (element IDs, `data-` attributes, API fields) documented in the README's "Customising the UI" section must keep working.
- Translation is machine-generated by OpenAI; the QA report checks completeness, consistency and fit, not linguistic quality.
- Languages and their direction come from `languages.py`; Arabic is right-to-left and needs an Arabic-capable font.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is **Rosetta**. The logo mark, colours and typography are open to replacement.
- Must not read as a generic SaaS / AI-startup template (gradients, glow, vague claims).

## Evidence on Hand

- The built-in synthetic sample: an original 3-page grade-4 lesson on equivalent fractions (`samples/`), with three generated images. It is the only document that may appear in screenshots, demos and copy.
- No customers, testimonials, usage numbers, benchmarks or pricing exist. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real result, not a claim about it: the sample and the side-by-side preview are the proof.
2. Be honest about limits: say what was changed, what is flagged, and what the tool does not check.
3. One clear next step on every screen and in every error.
4. Calm under waiting: progress is legible, and nothing distracts from checking a translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, visible focus, reduced-motion support, light and dark themes, layouts from 375px to 1440px, and correct right-to-left rendering for Arabic.
