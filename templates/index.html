How it worksSeconds to a few minutes per document
  1. 1

    Upload

    An .idml exported from InDesign (File › Export › InDesign Markup), or a .zip with its Links folder.

  2. 2

    Choose languages

    Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Arabic. Arabic stories are set right to left with an Arabic font included.

  3. 3

    Check and download

    Read the marked proof in your browser, then download a package that opens in InDesign, plus a QA report.

Before you rely on it

  • Translations are machine-made. The QA report checks that everything was translated, consistent and fits, not that the wording is right. Have a fluent speaker read it before print.
  • Your text is sent to OpenAI to be translated, so don't upload anything confidential unless that's acceptable to you.
  • Fit is measured per piece of text with open fonts that match your fonts' widths closely. InDesign's own line breaks can still differ slightly.
  • Rosetta has been tested on a limited set of documents. Unusual features such as footnotes or text on a path may not be handled.
